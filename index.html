Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of services including residential painting, commercial painting, interior and exterior painting, portrait drawings, pencil sketches, custom artwork, wall murals, and digital art.

How do I request a quote?

You can request a quote by calling us at 079 389 5048, emailing shortmane645@gmail.com, or filling out the contact form on our website. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes, we specialize in both residential and commercial projects. We have experience working with offices, retail spaces, restaurants, hotels, and industrial buildings.

Can I request a custom artwork design?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom artwork. Share your ideas and preferences with us, and we'll create a unique piece that matches your vision perfectly.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in South Africa and serve clients throughout the region. Contact us to discuss your project location and we'll be happy to assist.
